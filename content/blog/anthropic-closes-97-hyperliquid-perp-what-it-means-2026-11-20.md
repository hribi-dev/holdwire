**Short version:** Anthropic's shares opened on Nasdaq at $111.50 on Thursday and closed at $97.17, 17% above the $83 IPO price, The National reports. On Wednesday, the pre-IPO perpetual contract on Hyperliquid had ended near $98. So the crypto market's guess turned out much closer to the closing price than the IPO price did, but it badly missed the opening spike.

## What happened

Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83 and raised $107.3 billion in total, including a $26 billion private placement, according to The National. That is the largest IPO on record, ahead of SpaceX's $86 billion listing in June.

Trading under the ticker ANT started on Thursday, 19 November. The stock opened at $111.50, 34% above the IPO price, and rose as high as $115.10, according to Forbes. It then slid most of the day. CoinDesk reported it at $95.50 shortly after midday New York time. It closed at $97.17. At that price Anthropic is worth about $1.75 trillion on a fully diluted basis, The National reports.

Our [story yesterday](/blog/anthropic-ipo-hyperliquid-pre-ipo-perp-what-it-means-2026-11-19) explained the pre-IPO contract. Trade.xyz's ANT perp on [Hyperliquid](/hyperliquid) ended Wednesday at $98.33 and traded between $97.33 and $102 early on Thursday, before Nasdaq opened, according to the Perpys tracker. Open positions across the three Anthropic contracts on Hyperliquid were about $376 million.

## Why it matters

Pre-IPO perps are a new kind of market, and this was the biggest test so far. The result is mixed:

- **The close was close.** The perp at about $98 to $100 sat within roughly 1% to 3% of the $97.17 closing price. The IPO price of $83 was 17% below it.
- **The open was not.** Anyone short the perp near $100 faced a jump to $111.50 and above at the open, about 11% to 15% against them within minutes. With leverage, that is enough to wipe out a position.
- **The day was volatile.** The stock closed 13% below its opening price. A perp tracking it went through the same swing.

We could not find reported liquidation figures for the ANT perp on the first trading day, so we can't say how many traders were caught.

It also happened on a bad day for markets overall. The Nasdaq and the Dow were both down about 2% by late Thursday afternoon and bitcoin dropped to its lowest since early July, CoinDesk reports. PitchBook analyst Harrison Rolfes told The National that orders were about six times the shares on offer, and that the first year will test the price.

After the listing, the ANT perp converts to a standard per-share contract that follows the listed stock, Perpys explains. It warns that the switch from a market-set price to an external reference price can move the mark in one step.

## What this means for you

- **A perp is a price bet, not a share.** It pays out in USDC on price changes. "You cannot buy Anthropic shares on Hyperliquid," as Perpys puts it. If you want to own the company, a regulated broker that sells the listed stock is the direct route.
- **One good call is not a track record.** The perp tracked the close well this time but missed the open by more than 10%. Results across past listings have been mixed.
- **Leverage plus a listing day is a hard mix.** Prices can gap far past your stop on day one.
- **EU rules on leverage differ by country.** Check what your local regulator allows for retail traders before using any derivative. Our [MiCA explainer](/learn/mica-explained) covers what EU crypto rules do and do not protect.
