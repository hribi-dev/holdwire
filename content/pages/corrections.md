# Corrections

We aim to get things right first time. When we don't, we correct quickly and openly.

- **Report an error:** [corrections@domain]. Include the page link and what's wrong.
- **Response time:** we review reports within [2 working days].
- **How we correct:** factual errors are fixed in the article, with a dated note at the end saying what changed. Typos and formatting fixes are made without a note.
- **Significant errors** that change the meaning of a story are noted at the top of the article.
- **Data errors** in trackers or price pages are fixed at the source and logged on the tracker's changelog.

We do not quietly delete published articles. If we take one down for legal reasons, the page says so.
