**Spot gold traded at $3,877.66 an ounce early on Monday, 16 November, little changed after falling 4.5% last week to its lowest level in more than a year, according to a Business Times report cited by ChainCatcher.**

December US gold futures were down 0.2% at $3,880.60. Gold ended Friday near $3,883, below $3,900, and silver fell 2.63% that day to $48.63, Mitrade reports.

The pressure comes from US interest rates. CME FedWatch data showed a 92% chance of a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate hike at the 8–9 December meeting and an 8% chance of a half-point hike, ChainCatcher reports. Mitrade points to US producer prices rising 6.5% from a year earlier in October, the fastest pace since November 2022, and a 10-year Treasury yield near 5.15%. Gold pays no interest, so higher yields make it less attractive to hold.

Traders were waiting for remarks from Fed chair Kevin Warsh on Monday and for the minutes of the Fed's October meeting on Wednesday.

For EU readers: gold is quoted in dollars, so the price you see in euros also depends on the euro-dollar exchange rate. If you hold physical gold or a gold ETC, check the euro price, not just the dollar headline.
