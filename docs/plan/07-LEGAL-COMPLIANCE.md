# 07 — Legal & compliance checklist

> Claude drafts documents; **a lawyer licensed in Slovenia reviews them**. Claude is not a lawyer and this is not legal advice.

## Entity & admin [HRIBI]
- [ ] Decide entity: new d.o.o. (recommended for liability and clean separation from BalkanPay) vs. existing company.
- [ ] Bank account, invoicing, VAT registration (EU B2B reverse-charge for foreign advertisers; check thresholds).
- [ ] Accounting setup that handles crypto receipts if accepted.
- [ ] Check whether the site must be entered in the Slovenian media register (*razvid medijev*, Ministry of Culture) under the Mass Media Act (ZMed) and what that implies (impressum, editor-in-chief, labeling of paid content).

## Website legal pages [CLAUDE drafts → lawyer reviews]
- [ ] Impressum / About (company, address, editor-in-chief, contact)
- [ ] Terms of use
- [ ] Privacy policy (GDPR)
- [ ] Cookie policy + consent banner (consent before analytics/ads cookies; TCF v2.2 CMP once ads run)
- [ ] Financial disclaimer ("not investment advice", risk warnings)
- [ ] Editorial policy, corrections policy, AI-use statement
- [ ] Sponsored content & advertising policy; "How we make money"
- [ ] Conflicts of interest / holdings disclosure policy
- [ ] Contributor agreement (IP assignment/licence)

## Advertising & financial promotion
- [ ] Sponsored content clearly identifiable as advertising (EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive; Slovenian consumer protection + media law).
- [ ] **MiCA** (crypto): marketing communications for crypto-asset services/white papers must be fair, clear, not misleading, and identifiable as marketing. Only promote CASPs authorised in the EU when targeting EU users. Lawyer to confirm our obligations as a publisher vs. the sponsor's.
- [ ] Investment products/brokers: only licensed entities; CFD risk warnings where applicable.
- [ ] Advertiser contract: sponsor warrants accuracy and licensing; indemnity; our right to edit/reject/remove; mandatory `rel="sponsored"`; no ranking guarantees.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosures on pages with affiliate links.

## Data & IP
- [ ] Data API terms allow commercial display (CoinGecko/CMC/stock data).
- [ ] Image rights: licensed stock, own photos, or press-kit images with permission. Never scrape watch/auction photos.
- [ ] Trademarks: brand-name check (EUIPO/SIPO) before committing to the name.

## Ongoing
- [ ] Quarterly compliance check of sponsored inventory.
- [ ] Takedown/complaint process (contact + response SLA).
