**Visa will accept Western Union's USDPT stablecoin as a way to settle payments on its network, The Block reported on 12 November, in a tie-up with Anchorage Digital, the US crypto bank that issues the token.**

Under the deal, Visa and eligible partners can use USDPT to settle what they owe each other on VisaNet, the network behind Visa card payments. Anchorage and Visa have also set up a framework to consider other stablecoins that Anchorage issues, according to The Block. Fireblocks provides the wallet and settlement technology.

USDPT launched in May 2026 on the Solana blockchain. Western Union first rolled it out in the Philippines and Bolivia and plans to use it for round-the-clock settlement across its network in more than 200 countries. Visa said in September that its stablecoin settlement was running at $20 billion a year, more than 15 times the level a year earlier, The Block reports.

For people in the EU, nothing changes at the till: this is about how banks and payment firms settle with Visa behind the scenes, not about paying with a stablecoin. It is another sign that stablecoins are moving into the plumbing of card payments, even as total stablecoin supply has slipped to about $280 billion from $300 billion in March, according to The Block.
