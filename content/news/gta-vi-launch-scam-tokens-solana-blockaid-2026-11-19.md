**More than 20,000 tokens named after Grand Theft Auto appeared on Solana between 4 July and 4 November, and security firm Blockaid flagged over 8,800 of them as malicious, risky or spam.**

The game, GTA VI, is due out today, 19 November. Blockaid says about 46% of the flagged Solana tokens came from a pump.fun-style launchpad. It also found 593 GTA-named tokens on Base and 111 on Ethereum, some carrying hidden backdoors. In one case on 3 November, a buyer lost their whole balance of a GTA-themed token about a minute after buying it.

The bigger risk may be fake shops. Blockaid found cloned store pages offering pre-orders and an "Ultimate Edition" for $89.99, where every wallet option in the connect window led to the same wallet drainer. It counted 451 GTA-themed lure posts from 116 accounts linking to malicious sites. Speculative trading in tokens such as WSTAR, WASTED and LUCIA picked up ahead of the launch, KuCoin's daily report notes.

Blockaid's advice: there is no official GTA token or airdrop, and the official game stores don't take crypto or ask you to connect a wallet. Treat "check your eligibility" prompts as a red flag and read what you sign.
