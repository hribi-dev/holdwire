# Sponsored content & advertising policy

## How you can tell
Anything paid for is labelled, every time:
- **Sponsored**: an article paid for by a partner. It sits in our Sponsored section and carries the sponsor's name at the top.
- **Press release**: text supplied by a company, published as received after a basic check. It sits in our Press Releases section.
- **Advertisement**: display or native ads, labelled "Advertisement".
- **Affiliate links**: some comparison pages link to providers who pay us if you sign up. These pages say so at the top.

Sponsored articles and press releases never appear in our news feeds or "top stories" without their label.

## Links
All links in sponsored content, press releases, ads and affiliate placements carry `rel="sponsored"`. We do not sell links that pass search ranking value, and we do not promise search rankings to anyone.

## Who we accept
We accept partners that:
- are registered companies we can identify;
- hold the licences their service requires where our readers live. For crypto-asset services offered in the EU, that means authorisation under MiCA.

We decline, among others:
- promises of guaranteed or "risk-free" returns;
- token presales or offers built on urgency, and projects with anonymous teams and no working product;
- unlicensed brokers, exchanges or investment schemes;
- gambling presented as investing;
- anything we can't verify.

## Our editing rights
We edit sponsored content for clarity, accuracy and compliance. We may add risk warnings, decline a piece, or remove it later if facts change. The sponsor is responsible for the accuracy of its claims and for its licensing.

## Separation from the newsroom
Buying sponsored content or advertising does not buy editorial coverage, favourable or otherwise.
