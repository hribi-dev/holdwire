**The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) is now accepting applications from wholesale banks that want permission to carry out regulated crypto activities, and recommends they apply by 28 February 2027, ahead of the new regime starting on 25 October 2027, TokenPost reports.**

Banks apply through a "variation of permission", which adds activities to the licence they already hold. The February date is a recommendation, not a formal deadline, according to TokenPost. Banks that apply later but before 25 October 2027 enter a transition period: they can keep serving existing contracts but cannot sign new ones with UK customers until approved.

The step is part of the FCA's finalised crypto rules. The FCA and the Bank of England also plan a joint roadmap for tokenisation in wholesale markets, setting target dates for their work; Mondo Visione reported in October that it was due in November.

For EU readers, the UK sits outside MiCA, so a UK crypto permission does not allow a firm to serve customers in the EU, and an EU licence does not cover the UK. The UK is building its own rulebook on a later timetable than the EU, whose transition ended on 1 July 2026.
