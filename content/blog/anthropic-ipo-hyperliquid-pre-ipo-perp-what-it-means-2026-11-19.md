**Short version:** Anthropic priced its stock market listing at $83 a share on Wednesday, above the $70 to $78 range it had marketed. A "pre-IPO" perpetual contract on Hyperliquid was trading near $98 at the same time, about 18% higher, according to Bitcoin.com News. Today, when ANT starts trading on Nasdaq, that contract stops floating on its own and starts following the real share price, so the gap has to close one way or the other.

## What happened

Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83, up from the 825 million it first planned, raising $81.34 billion before fees, Bitcoin.com News reports. Together with a $26 billion private placement, the deal tops $94 billion and values the company at more than $1.5 trillion, according to Bloomberg as cited by CoinDesk. That makes it the largest stock market listing on record, ahead of SpaceX in June. Trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT is set to start today, 19 November.

Crypto traders had been betting on the price for weeks. Trade.xyz, one of the teams that list markets on Hyperliquid (see our [Hyperliquid hub](/hyperliquid)), launched an ANT perpetual contract on 26 October, the day Anthropic filed for its listing. It opened near $138, according to Crypto Briefing. On 18 November it traded $72.5 million in 24 hours, with $136.3 million in open positions.

After the $83 price was set, the contract still traded near $98 at 4:35 p.m. New York time, Bitcoin.com News reports. So traders on Hyperliquid expect ANT to open well above its IPO price. The listing price only decides what IPO buyers pay; the first trade on Nasdaq can be very different.

## Why it matters

A [perpetual contract](/glossary) is a bet on a price, settled in cash. It gives no shares, no votes and no dividends. Normally a funding payment pulls a perp towards a reference price. Before a listing there is no reference price, so Trade.xyz runs funding at 1% of its usual rate and lets the market set the level, according to The Motley Fool. After the first regular Nasdaq session, the contract switches to tracking the stock.

That switch is the risk. Anyone long at $98 loses if ANT trades lower, and leveraged positions can be liquidated on day one. Track records are mixed. Grayscale found that pre-IPO contracts called the direction of the opening correctly in seven of eight listings from May to October, with Shein the exception, Bitcoin.com News reports. The SpaceX perp was quoted at $157.59 before the shares opened at $150, according to The Motley Fool.

Crypto platforms also sold token versions of the stock. Binance ran a pre-IPO sale of an ANT token from xStocks that hit its 500 million USDC cap within hours, CoinDesk reports. Binance says it does not verify the reserves or guarantee one-to-one backing. Ondo is launching its own token, ANTon, for eligible investors outside the US, The Block reports. These tokens follow the share price but are not the shares themselves.

## What this means for you

- **A perp is not a share.** It pays out on the price only. If you want to own Anthropic, a regulated broker that sells the listed stock is the direct route.
- **Expect sharp moves today.** The gap between $83 and the perp price will close fast, and leverage turns that into forced selling for whoever is on the wrong side.
- **Read the small print on tokens.** Who holds the real shares, can you redeem, and is the issuer allowed to sell to people in your country? Our [MiCA explainer](/learn/mica-explained) covers what EU rules protect, and what they don't.
- **Look up your local rules.** Leveraged derivatives can be restricted for retail investors in some EU countries.

## What to watch

- ANT's opening price on Nasdaq today, and how far the Hyperliquid contract moves to meet it.
- Liquidations on the ANT perp during the first session.
- Whether traders keep using the Hyperliquid contract once the real stock trades. Nvidia perps there stayed busy around news this month (see our [Nvidia perps story](/blog/nvidia-perps-hyperliquid-huang-exit-what-it-means-2026-11-13)).
- Reserve reports from xStocks and Ondo for their ANT tokens.
