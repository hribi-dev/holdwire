**Payward, the private company that owns the Kraken exchange, reported "total platform margin" of $487 million for the third quarter, 12% lower than a year earlier but 12% higher than in the second quarter, CoinDesk reports.**

The figure is new. Payward now leads with platform margin instead of adjusted revenue, because it also subtracts staking rewards paid out to clients, Unchained reports. On the old measure, third-quarter adjusted revenue was $553 million, down 15% from a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA, a rough measure of operating profit, was $60 million, up from $23 million in the second quarter but well below $181 million a year ago. Neither measure is audited, Unchained notes.

Trading picked up from the summer: platform transactions were $353 billion, 22% more than in the second quarter and 30% less than a year earlier. Funded accounts reached 7.1 million. Income tied to client assets and access to products, rather than trading, made up 52% of platform margin, up from 46% a year before, according to CoinDesk.

The slowdown is industry-wide. Coinbase's third-quarter revenue fell 35% from a year earlier and Gemini's fell 19%, CoinDesk reports. Payward added artificial intelligence as a fifth business line, with plans for a personal finance agent. For EU users, its xStocks tokenized-share platform added more than 200 UK and EU stocks this week, according to Unchained. Payward filed confidentially for a US stock market listing in November 2025.
