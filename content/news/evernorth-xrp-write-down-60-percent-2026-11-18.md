**Evernorth, the Nasdaq-listed XRP treasury company backed by Ripple, carried its 346 million XRP at $342 million at the end of September, down from an original recorded value of $847 million.**

The company's first quarterly report, filed on 13 November, shows $271 million of impairment charges in the first nine months of 2026, CoinDesk reports. Added to $234 million from its 2025 accounts, that makes $505 million, or nearly 60% of the original value of about $2.44 per token.

The book value is lower than the market value was at the time. XRP closed September near $1.50 on Coinbase, which would have valued the holdings at about $516 million, according to CoinDesk. Evernorth records its XRP at cost less impairment, and the filing says the reduced value is not raised again if the price recovers. Gains are only recognised on a sale.

Evernorth reported a nine-month net loss of $32.9 million, after a $240.3 million non-cash gain on investor advances offset much of the impairment, Finbold reports. XRP has since fallen further, to about $0.96 on Tuesday, below the $0.99 average value at which the tokens were carried in September, CoinDesk reports. CoinDesk said it had asked Evernorth for comment.

For EU readers, the case shows how much a crypto treasury company's reported numbers can depend on accounting rules, and why book value and market value can differ widely.
