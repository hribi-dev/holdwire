# Conflicts of interest & disclosures

Our writers and editors may own crypto-assets or have business ties in the industry we cover. We think readers should know.

## Rules
- Staff list relevant holdings and roles here, and keep the list current.
- Any article about a company, protocol or asset that a writer or editor holds, works for or advises carries a disclosure line at the top.
- Nobody may write about an asset they plan to trade around publication.
