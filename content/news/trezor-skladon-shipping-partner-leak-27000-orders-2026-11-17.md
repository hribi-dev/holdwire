**Hardware wallet maker Trezor says attackers took customer details from about 27,000 orders after breaking into Skladon, one of its shipping partners, its second shipping-partner leak since August, The Block reports.**

Trezor disclosed the breach in a blog post on Sunday, 15 November. The attacker is believed to have exported data on orders placed between 9 August and 7 November, including full names, phone numbers, email and delivery addresses, and order details. Trezor says its own systems were not affected and that no wallet backups, PINs or passphrases leaked. Some customers have already had scam calls from people pretending to be Trezor support, and the company warned of "potential risks to your physical security", according to The Block.

Trezor is reviewing its partners, cutting how long it keeps order data from 90 to 45 days, and has introduced anonymous pickup in 18 European countries so buyers can collect orders without giving a name or home address. In August, a breach at another partner, ShipMonk, exposed details of about 13,700 customers.

If you bought a Trezor in that period: Trezor will never call you or ask for your recovery phrase. Ignore calls, emails or letters asking you to "verify" or "update" your device, and never type your seed words into a website or app. A leaked address is also a reason to be discreet about holding crypto at home.
