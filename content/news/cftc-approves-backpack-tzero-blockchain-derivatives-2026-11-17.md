**The US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has approved crypto exchange Backpack and tZERO to run regulated derivatives exchanges and clearing houses that use blockchain to execute and settle trades, The Block reported on Monday, 16 November.**

Backpack's US exchange, TLD Markets, was approved as a designated contract market on 16 November, and its clearing arm was registered as a derivatives clearing organisation on 13 November, according to approval documents cited by Bloomingbit. Backpack plans 24-hour leveraged crypto perpetual futures for US users, with fiat and crypto accepted as collateral. tZERO got its exchange approval the week before, after an earlier clearing approval, The Block reports.

CFTC chair Michael Selig, currently the agency's only sitting commissioner, called it a regulated pathway for market infrastructure "built using blockchain technology". Backpack's BP token traded at $1.19 on Tuesday morning, up about 42% in 24 hours, Bloomingbit reports, citing CoinMarketCap.

Backpack, founded by former FTX and Alameda staff, bought FTX's European arm in January 2025, BNN Bloomberg reported at the time. For EU readers, the US approval does not cover Backpack's European business, which falls under EU rules. Regulated US perpetuals are a product US traders have so far mostly found offshore or on decentralised venues such as Hyperliquid.
