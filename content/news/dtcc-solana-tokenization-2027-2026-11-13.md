**The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC), which settles most US stock and bond trades, said on 12 November that it plans to connect its tokenization service to the Solana blockchain in the second half of 2027.**

The DTCC Tokenization Service launched on 28 October for eligible clients. It lets assets held at the Depository Trust Company (DTC) be turned into tokens and back again. According to DTCC, tokens on Solana will keep the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards as the underlying securities. The release does not say which assets will go to Solana first. For the service as a whole, eligible assets are limited to Russell 1000 stocks, US Treasury bills, notes and bonds, and ETFs that track major indexes, according to Unchained.

Solana is the fifth network on DTCC's roadmap, Unchained reports, after the Canton Network and DTCC's own private network (both live at launch), Stellar (planned for the first half of 2027) and Circle's Arc blockchain (2027). DTCC says the plan can still change. This is a plan, not a live service.

For EU readers, nothing changes yet: DTC holds US securities, and access runs through DTC's institutional clients, not retail apps. It does show the biggest US market plumbing moving onto public blockchains, which follows the [New York Stock Exchange's Avalanche plans](/blog/nyse-avalanche-tokenized-stocks-what-it-means-2026-11-10) earlier this week.
