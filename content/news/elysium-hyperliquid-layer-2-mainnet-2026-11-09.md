**Elysium, a layer 2 network built for the Hyperliquid ecosystem by liquid-staking protocol Kinetiq, is scheduled to launch its mainnet on 9 November, according to Kinetiq.**

Elysium is a separate chain that settles to HyperEVM, Hyperliquid's smart-contract layer. It is meant to give trading apps, launchpads and automated market makers more room than HyperEVM itself. Fees are paid in HYPE, bridged one-to-one, so users don't need a new token, Bankless reports. Elysium has no token or points programme yet.

Bankless says the Nest DEX should be live from the first block, with the Speculate prediction market, the Dawn DEX and two launchpads also lined up. Half of sequencer fees will go to buying back Kinetiq's KNTQ token, and Kinetiq says 25% of sequencer revenue is set aside for builders whose apps bring usage.

The launch version is early. Bankless reports that at the start one sequencer orders transactions and a whitelisted set of validators checks state, with permissionless fraud proofs promised later. Hooks that let apps read Hyperliquid's order books arrive about a month after launch. Kinetiq's documentation warns details may still change.

For readers, that means a new chain with new bridges and young apps. Bridging funds always adds smart-contract risk, so start small if you try it.
