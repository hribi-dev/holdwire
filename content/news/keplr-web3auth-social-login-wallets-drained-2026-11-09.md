**At least $6.01 million was taken from about 44,700 Keplr wallets that had been set up with Google or Apple sign-in, according to blockchain data firm Bitquery.**

Keplr is a popular wallet for Cosmos-based chains. Its social-login option relied on Web3Auth, which reported a security issue and paused its embedded wallet service on the evening of 6 November (UTC). Keplr confirmed unauthorised transactions early on 7 November and switched off Google and Apple login. Wallets created with a recovery phrase or a hardware device were not affected, Keplr says.

Bitquery traced thefts on Cosmos Hub, Osmosis, Celestia, Injective and other chains. Most losses were small: the median was $1.34 and 88% of victims lost under $100, though the largest single loss was about $325,000, Blockonomi reports. Bitquery's total is a floor because unpriced tokens are excluded. Web3Auth has moved some at-risk funds to its own recovery wallets and published those addresses. Neither company has yet explained how the keys were exposed.

If you used Keplr's Google or Apple login: don't send new funds to that account, move what's left to a new wallet with its own recovery phrase, and ignore anyone offering to "recover" your money. Only trust updates from Keplr's official channels.
