**Revolut said on 4 November that it has completed a fundraising round valuing it at $115 billion, according to the company's statement published by Disruption Banking.**

The round was led by Glade Brook Capital Partners, Coatue, Durable Capital Partners, T. Rowe Price and Baillie Gifford. Revolut did not say how much money was raised or whether the deal was mainly new shares or existing shares changing hands. Chief executive Nik Storonsky said employees were again given a chance to share in the value they have created.

The figures in the announcement are Revolut's own. It says it has more than 80 million retail customers, and that in 2025 revenue rose 46% to $6.0 billion and pre-tax profit 57% to $2.3 billion. This year it has launched banks in the UK, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand, won banking licences in France, Colombia and Argentina, and received conditional approval for a US national bank charter, FinTech Global reports. It already runs banking operations across 30 European Economic Area markets.

For EU customers, a bigger valuation changes nothing about your account. What matters is which licensed company holds each part of your money. Bank deposits, investments and crypto are often run by different entities with different protections, and crypto holdings are not covered by bank deposit guarantees. The app's legal documents say which entity you deal with.
