**Upbit, South Korea's largest crypto exchange by trading volume, is listing HYPE, the token of the Hyperliquid exchange, with trading pairs against the Korean won, bitcoin and USDT from 15:00 Korean time (07:00 CET) on 10 November, according to Coinfomania and The Defiant.**

The Defiant reports that HYPE rose 1.4% in the 35 minutes around the announcement, outpacing bitcoin over the same window. Coinfomania, which cites a post by WuBlockchain, did not report a price move and noted that early trading on new listings can be volatile.

The won pair is the significant part. Most retail trading in South Korea happens in won markets, so a KRW listing on Upbit gives Korean investors direct access to HYPE without first buying another coin. Hyperliquid is the largest decentralised exchange for perpetual futures by trading volume, and HYPE has recently been added by other large platforms: The Defiant links earlier coverage of a HYPE jump when Robinhood announced a spot listing.

For readers in the EU, the listing does not change where HYPE is available here. New listings on big exchanges often bring sharp swings in the first hours, in both directions, so a jump around the listing time says little about what comes next.
