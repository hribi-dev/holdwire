**Nvidia's perpetual futures on Hyperliquid fell as much as 10% on Wednesday evening, to a low of about $156.14, after a Wall Street Journal report that Nvidia's board was preparing to remove chief executive Jensen Huang, The Block reports. Open interest in the market has since reached a record $535 million.**

The slide started around 17:00 New York time, after the regular stock market had closed. By 19:50 the NVDA-USDC perp was down 6.8% over 24 hours at $158.86, The Block says. Nvidia's Nasdaq-listed shares fell more than 8% in after-hours trading, to $159.51, CBS News reports. Nvidia had not replied to The Block by publication. On prediction markets, Kalshi put the odds of Huang stepping down before 13 November at 64% and Polymarket at 71%, according to The Block.

Trading in the market, one of the HIP-3 markets that outside teams launch on Hyperliquid, has grown fast. Open interest rose 5.8 times in seven days and daily volume passed $1 billion for the first time, according to on-chain data reported by BlockBeats. That makes NVDA the largest HIP-3 market and the fourth-largest on Hyperliquid, behind BTC, ETH and HYPE. Of 9,517 trading wallets, 83% were long, and those longs were sitting on unrealized losses of about $14.6 million.

These are leveraged derivatives that track Nvidia's price around the clock, not shares. They let traders react when Nasdaq is closed, but positions can be liquidated quickly in a move like this one.
