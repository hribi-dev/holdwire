**PM Derivatives, an affiliate of prediction market Polymarket, has been approved by the National Futures Association (NFA) to operate as a futures commission merchant in the US, Finance Magnates reported on 3 November.**

## What happened

A futures commission merchant is a broker that can take customer orders and hold customer money for futures-style trading. The approval completes the third part of Polymarket's US setup: Polymarket US runs the exchange, QC Clearing handles clearing, and PM Derivatives now acts as the broker.

For now, PM Derivatives will only offer fully funded trades: customers pay the full cost of a position when they place it. Offering margin, which lets traders borrow to take bigger positions, needs further approval from the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), according to KuCoin's daily report and Finance Magnates. Existing brokers that already connect customers to Polymarket US keep their access.

## Context

The approval landed on US election day, when prediction markets were in the spotlight. Polymarket's odds of Democrats winning both chambers of Congress rose from about 75% to 99% overnight as results came in, CryptoTicker reported. Our [blog post on the result](/blog/democrats-win-congress-what-it-means-for-crypto-2026-11-04) covers what it means for crypto.

## What it means for EU readers

Polymarket US is for US customers. Several EU countries restrict or block prediction markets under gambling law, so check your local rules before using any such platform.
