**Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms has signed a $28 billion, 15-year lease for its entire Corsicana, Texas campus with a frontier AI lab, and its shares rose about 14% on Wednesday, according to CoinDesk.**

## What happened

The lease covers 910 MW of computing capacity on the 332-acre site, with average rent of about $1.8 billion a year, according to Riot's third-quarter report. It could be worth about $54 billion if two five-year extensions are used. Capacity is due in three phases between September 2027 and July 2028. The tenant was described only as a frontier AI lab.

The rest of the sector went the other way. Hut 8 and Cipher Mining fell more than 20%, and CleanSpark, Core Scientific, Galaxy Digital and IREN each dropped more than 10%, CoinDesk says. Earlier in the day, AI data-centre stocks had sold off after Democratic wins in Texas and Ohio. Tesla and SpaceX shares fell too.

## Context

Several large miners are turning their sites and power contracts into data centres for AI companies. Riot has been selling bitcoin to pay for the build-out: it sold 20,264 BTC this year for about $1.5 billion and held 2,418 at the end of September. Revenue from mining fell to $116 million in the quarter from $160.8 million a year earlier.

## What it means for EU readers

Mining shares are increasingly a bet on AI infrastructure, not just on the bitcoin price. If you hold them through a broker, they can move very differently from bitcoin itself, as Wednesday showed.
