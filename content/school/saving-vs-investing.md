## Two different jobs

**Saving** means keeping money safe so it's there when you need it. The goal is safety, not growth.

**Investing** means putting money into things that can grow over time, such as shares, funds or, for some people, crypto. The goal is growth, and the price is risk: the value can fall, sometimes a lot, sometimes for years.

The simple rule: **the sooner you might need the money, the safer it should be.**

## Emergency fund first

Before investing, most guides suggest building an **emergency fund**: commonly three to six months of essential costs (rent, food, bills) kept in a savings account you can reach quickly.

Why first? Because life happens. If your car breaks down and all your money is in investments that just fell 30%, you'd have to sell at a loss. An emergency fund means you never have to sell investments at a bad time.

## How safe is "safe"?

In the EU, bank deposits are protected by national deposit guarantee schemes up to **€100,000 per person, per bank**. If a bank fails, that amount is paid back.

Things that are **not** covered by this guarantee:

- Shares and funds (these have different investor protection rules)
- Crypto held on an exchange
- Money in apps that aren't banks, unless they keep it in a protected bank account

That doesn't mean crypto exchanges are all unsafe. It means the protection is different, which is why the exchange you choose matters. Level 2 covers how to check.

## A simple order

1. Pay off expensive debt (credit cards, overdrafts).
2. Build an emergency fund.
3. Invest money you won't need for at least five years, and only what you can live with seeing fall.
