**OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on 13 November, letting its always-on "dots" agents buy things for users with their payment cards, built together with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, according to PPC Land.**

The wallet does not hand the real card number to the merchant or the agent. After the user approves a purchase, the card network issues a payment token, a stand-in card number that is charged to the card the user picked, PPC Land reports. Every purchase needs a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint, and the user sees the items, the total and the card before approving. Card rewards and dispute protection still apply.

Fraud-prevention firms Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd, and payment providers Adyen, Checkout.com and Stripe, are working with OpenAI so that agent purchases are not flagged as fraud. Fully delegated buying, with spending limits and categories set in advance, is described as a later step, with no date.

For now it is only for US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 or over with eligible US-issued cards. There is no word on a European launch, and the wallet uses ordinary cards, not crypto or stablecoins. The same card networks are also testing stablecoins: this week Visa added USDPT as a settlement option ([our brief](/news/visa-western-union-usdpt-stablecoin-settlement-2026-11-13)).
