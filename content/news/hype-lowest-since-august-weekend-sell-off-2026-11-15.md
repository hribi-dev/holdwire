**Hyperliquid's HYPE token traded at $62.49 early on Sunday, 15 November, down 12.4% in 24 hours and at its lowest level since August, according to CoinGecko data cited by CryptoTicker.**

The move reverses Friday, when HYPE was the only top-ten coin trading higher as bitcoin fell below $70,000 (we [covered that here](/news/hype-only-top-ten-gainer-q3-revenue-2026-11-14)). Over the weekend the broader sell-off caught up with it. On Kraken, HYPE closed Saturday at $64.30, down 10.3%, after an intraday low of $60.00, CryptoTicker reports. It is now about 36% below its September record of $97.96 and down about 18% on the week.

CryptoTicker found no Hyperliquid-specific trigger. The token fell with the market, but harder than bitcoin, as CoinGecko's perpetual-exchange and decentralised-exchange categories dropped about 13% each. In one hour on Saturday afternoon, $24.94 million of HYPE long positions were liquidated across three exchanges, TokenPost reported. Hyperliquid's buybacks, which use trading fees to buy HYPE on the market, had supported the price on Friday but were not enough over the weekend, CryptoTicker notes.

For EU readers: HYPE is still well above where it started 2026, but a 15% range in one day shows how volatile it is. If you trade it with leverage, check your liquidation price.
