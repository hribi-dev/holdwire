# How we make money

Holdwire is free to read. These are the ways we pay for it:

1. **Sponsored content and press releases.** Companies pay to publish labelled pieces in separate sections. [Policy](/advertising-policy)
2. **Advertising.** Display ads on some pages, always labelled.
3. **Newsletter sponsorships.** A labelled sponsor slot in our newsletters.
4. **Affiliate links.** On some comparison pages, a provider may pay us if you open an account through our link. It costs you nothing extra, and it doesn't change our rankings. We only link to providers licensed for your country.
5. **Data and research.** [Later: premium reports and data access.]

None of these buys editorial coverage. If a company pays us and is also in the news, our newsroom covers it like any other company and discloses the relationship.
