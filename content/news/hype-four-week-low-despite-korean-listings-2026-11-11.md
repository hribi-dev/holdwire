**HYPE, the token of the Hyperliquid exchange, traded around $74 on Tuesday, down 3.2% in 24 hours and about 13% over the week, according to 24/7 Wall St. TokenPost describes it as a four-week low.**

The drop came on the same day South Korea's two largest exchanges, Upbit and Bithumb, both opened HYPE trading against the Korean won. Upbit limited trading in the first minutes after launch and allowed only limit orders for the first two hours, 24/7 Wall St. reports. The listings did not stop the slide, which came as the wider crypto market fell after hotter US inflation data.

24/7 Wall St. points to other pressures. Two large holders sold $109 million of HYPE on 31 October, and US-listed Hyperliquid ETFs saw net outflows of $2.7 million on 9 November. Trades on Korean exchanges also do not generate fees for Hyperliquid's Assistance Fund, which buys back HYPE with exchange revenue. TokenPost notes that daily fees on Hyperliquid have recovered after an early-November dip.

HYPE is still up about 190% this year, against a 15% fall for bitcoin, and about 24.5% below its 23 September record of $98, according to 24/7 Wall St. Big swings in both directions are normal for it.
