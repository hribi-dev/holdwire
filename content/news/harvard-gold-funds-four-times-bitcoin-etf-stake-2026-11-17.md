**Harvard Management Company held $596.8 million in two gold funds at the end of September, more than four times its $144.1 million stake in BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT), according to its latest quarterly 13F filing reported by Bitcoin Magazine.**

Harvard kept 3,044,612 IBIT shares, unchanged since March. It has been shrinking that position since late 2025, when IBIT was briefly its largest disclosed holding at $442.9 million. Meanwhile it built up gold: SPDR Gold Shares, worth $442.2 million, became its third-largest holding, up from 58,862 shares at midyear, alongside $154.6 million in the iShares Gold Trust, Bitcoin Magazine reports.

The filing is a snapshot from 30 September and does not show trades since. Bitcoin has fallen about 23% since then, putting Harvard's IBIT stake at about $110.6 million on Monday, Bitcoin Magazine calculates. Gold has also fallen in recent weeks, trading near $3,862 an ounce on Monday, CoinDesk reported.

Other filings point the other way: Wells Fargo's reported IBIT holding more than doubled to 49.58 million shares, worth $2.35 billion, though The Defiant notes the filing does not show whether those shares are the bank's own or held for clients.

For EU readers, these US filings are one of the few public windows into how big institutions split money between gold and bitcoin, but they are three months old by the time they appear.
