**Payments made with stablecoins rose 45% to $482 billion in the first nine months of 2026, while total stablecoin supply grew just 0.4% to $305 billion, according to Allium data cited by CoinDesk.**

The number of payment transactions rose 71% to 1.04 billion, and September set a record of $72 billion, CoinDesk reports. Payments are getting smaller and more frequent: the average fell to $462 from $544. Standard Chartered estimates that stablecoins now change hands about 30 times a month, double last year's pace. Card programmes are one example: StraitsX says daily transactions on its cards rose from about 66,000 in January to 291,000 in October.

Supply is a different story. It has stayed between $300 billion and $320 billion for a year, and then fell about $6.7 billion in the week to 7 November, to $292 billion by DefiLlama's count, the steepest weekly drop of 2026, according to Cryptonews. Tether's USDT and Circle's USDC made up two-thirds of that fall. Standard Chartered has pushed back its $2 trillion stablecoin market forecast to 2030 from 2028, CoinDesk reports.

For EU readers, the takeaway is that stablecoins are being used more for spending and transfers even as fewer people park money in them. In the EU, check that the stablecoin you use comes from an issuer authorised under MiCA.
