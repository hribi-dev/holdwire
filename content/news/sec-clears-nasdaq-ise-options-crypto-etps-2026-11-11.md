**The US Securities and Exchange Commission has approved a Nasdaq ISE rule that lets the exchange list options on exchange-traded products holding "digital commodities", extending crypto ETP options beyond bitcoin and ether funds, Genfinity reports.**

The approval, dated 10 November, covers rule filing SR-ISE-2026-42, submitted in July. Once a fund qualifies, ISE can list options on it without asking the SEC to approve each product separately. To qualify, each crypto asset in the fund must have averaged at least $700 million in daily market value over the previous 12 months, and at least 85% of the fund's assets must be in coins with derivatives on markets that share surveillance data with the exchange, such as CME and Coinbase Derivatives.

Genfinity lists 19 assets that fall under the US "digital commodity" framework, including BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LINK and AVAX. The rule is a framework, not approval of any specific fund: each ETP still has to be listed under Nasdaq's standards.

Options let investors hedge or bet on price moves with less capital up front, which also means they can amplify losses. For EU readers, US-listed crypto ETPs and their options are generally not offered to retail investors here; EU investors use European-listed crypto ETPs instead.
