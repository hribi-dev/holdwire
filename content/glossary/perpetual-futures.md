---
title: Perpetual futures
short: A futures contract with no expiry date, kept close to the spot price by regular funding payments between long and short traders.
published: 2026-11-02
author: Holdwire
---
Perpetual futures ("perps") let traders bet on a price going up or down, usually with leverage, without ever settling the contract on a fixed date. They are the main product on exchanges like [Hyperliquid](/hyperliquid). See also [funding rate](/glossary/funding-rate) and [liquidation](/glossary/liquidation).
