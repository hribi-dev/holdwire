## 2. Rate card logic (to be filled with real numbers once traffic exists)
Crypto media price sponsored posts mostly on traffic and brand. Rough market reference: small/new sites €100–500, mid-size €1–5k, top-tier €10k+ per post. We start low, earn case studies, and raise prices each quarter as traffic grows.

Starting proposal (month 6, ~30–50k sessions/mo):
- Press release: €150–300
- Sponsored article: €500–1,200
- Premium bundle: €1,500–3,000
- Newsletter primary slot: €CPM-based (€30–60 CPM on opens, crypto-niche)
Claude recalculates every quarter from GA4/Plausible numbers.

## 4. Sponsor acceptance policy
**Accept:** registered companies; licensed financial services where licensing is required (MiCA CASP status for crypto-service providers serving the EU); legitimate projects with a real team or product.
**Reject:** guaranteed-return claims, anonymous teams with no product, presales/ICOs pushing urgency, unlicensed brokers, gambling dressed as investing, anything we can't verify.
**Required in every sponsored piece:** label, disclosure, risk warning for crypto/investment content, no misleading claims.

## 5. Sales process
1. **Warm network first** (Hribi): Solana ecosystem projects, Harmonic/HumidiFi circles, BalkanPay partners. Offer launch pricing / first 10 sponsors at a discount for testimonials.
2. **Inbound:** `/advertise` page + "Write for us / Partner with us" pages rank for those queries; the form feeds the CRM (Notion/HubSpot free).
3. **Outbound** (Claude prepares lists + emails; Hribi sends from his own address): projects that recently raised funding, exchanges entering the EU, fintechs launching products, watch dealers/auction houses.
4. **Agencies:** crypto PR agencies buy placements in bulk; give them a reseller rate.
5. Invoicing via Stripe/bank; crypto payments optional (USDC), but accounting must be clean.
