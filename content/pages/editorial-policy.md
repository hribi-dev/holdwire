# Editorial policy

## Independence
Our newsroom decides what we cover and what we conclude. Advertisers, sponsors and affiliate partners have no say over editorial content, and they never see it before it's published. Commercial staff do not direct editorial coverage.

## Accuracy and sourcing
- We cite primary sources wherever possible: regulators' registers and filings, official announcements, on-chain data and company documents.
- Every number is checked against its source before publishing, and every data point on the site shows where it comes from.
- Each guide shows who wrote it, who reviewed it, and when it was last updated. "Last updated" changes only when we make a real change.

## What we don't do
- We don't give personal investment advice and we don't publish price predictions as fact.
- We don't accept payment, tokens or gifts in exchange for editorial coverage.
- We don't use anonymous or invented author identities.

## Use of AI
We use AI tools to help with research, first drafts, data processing and fact-checking. A named human editor reviews every piece before publication and is responsible for it. We never publish AI output without that review.

## Reviews and comparisons
Rankings in our comparisons are based on criteria we publish on each page: licensing, fees, availability in your country, security and usability. Affiliate relationships do not change rankings. Pages with affiliate links say so at the top.

## Updates and corrections
When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. See our [corrections policy](/corrections).

## Conflicts of interest
Our staff disclose relevant holdings and business ties. See [disclosures](/disclosures).
