**TG Commodities, the issuer of the Tether Gold (XAUT) token, has received a clean audit opinion from KPMG U.S. confirming it held 520,089.350 troy ounces of gold, worth $2.246 billion, at the end of 2025, The Block reports.**

The audit, announced on 12 November, covers the year to 31 December 2025. KPMG physically inspected and counted every bar and checked each one's identifying details, according to The Block. The gold held was 0.050 ounces more than the 520,089.300 XAUT tokens in circulation, so every token was covered. By our calculation that is about 16 tonnes of gold, stored in Switzerland.

Each XAUT represents an ownership share in gold on specific bars, and the gold belongs to token holders, according to the issuer. crypto.news describes it as the token's first Big Four audit after five years of more limited disclosures. KPMG had already given a clean opinion on the 2025 accounts of Tether's USDT issuer in August, and Tether says it plans to audit its other businesses too.

For EU readers, an audit like this answers one key question for tokenized gold, whether the metal exists, but only as of the audit date. It does not change how XAUT is treated under [MiCA](/learn/mica-explained), and holding the token still means trusting the issuer and its custodian rather than holding bars yourself.
