**The $60,000 put has become the largest single bitcoin options position on Deribit, with about $900 million of open interest, overtaking the $100,000 call at about $867 million, CoinDesk reports.**

A put is a contract that pays out if the price falls below a set level, so traders often use it as insurance. The change at the top is less about a rush into crash bets than about traders closing their upside bets, Nicolas Quatravaux of Paradigm told CoinDesk. Paradigm data shows more than 12,000 BTC of $100,000 calls were bought in September and about 6,700 BTC of them sold back in October. Open interest is "a stock, not a flow", he said, so the $60,000 put reflects positions built over months.

One large trader has been moving a big put position to lower strike prices as bitcoin fell, from $74,000 in late October to $63,000, according to CoinDesk. Volmex's 30-day implied volatility index for bitcoin hit 57.13 on Sunday, its highest since June, before easing to 44.

Bitcoin traded at $64,644 early on Monday, down 0.32% in 24 hours, while total crypto market value fell 3.93% to $2.19 trillion, TokenPost reports.

For EU readers: options open interest shows where traders have positioned, not where the price will go. Options are complex, leveraged products that can lose value quickly.
