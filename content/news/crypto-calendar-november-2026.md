**November is busier than usual for crypto.** Here are the scheduled dates worth knowing, and why each could matter. Dates are as scheduled at the time of writing.

| Date | Event | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Nov | US midterm elections | Control of Congress shapes the next round of US crypto legislation |
| 6 Nov | US jobs report (non-farm payrolls) | Moves rate expectations, which tends to move risk assets |
| 10 Nov | US inflation data (CPI) | Usually the bigger market mover of the two |
| 15–17 Nov | Solana Breakpoint, Olympia London | Solana's flagship conference, in the UK for the first time; product and partnership news often lands here |
| 24 Nov | Monad team token unlock (reported) | Large unlocks add supply that can weigh on a token's price |

## How to use a calendar like this

- **Expect volatility around data releases**, especially if you trade with leverage. Funding rates and liquidations tend to spike around them.
- **Unlocks are known in advance**, but the market reaction isn't. Check the size of an unlock relative to the tokens already circulating.
- **Conference news is often already priced in** by the time it is announced.
