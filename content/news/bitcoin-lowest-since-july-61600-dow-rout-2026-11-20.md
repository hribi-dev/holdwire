**Bitcoin fell to around $61,600 on Thursday, 19 November, its lowest price since early July, according to investingLive, and is on track for a seventh weekly loss in a row.**

It traded near $63,900 when US markets opened, then slid through the day as stocks fell. CoinDesk recorded $61,800 at 3:37 p.m. New York time, when the Nasdaq and the Dow were both down 2.1% and the S&P 500 1.9%. The Dow lost more than 1,000 points and the VIX volatility index rose above 28, investingLive reports. Ether fell below $1,800.

The backdrop is the same as all week: minutes of the Fed's October meeting showed most officials favour more rate rises, and some are open to a half-point move in December. Higher rates tend to weigh on assets that pay no interest. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost more than $300 million on Wednesday, taking November outflows to about $2.6 billion, according to investingLive. The Crypto Fear and Greed Index read 10, deep in "extreme fear".

Gold fell too, down 2% to $3,667 an ounce on Thursday, CoinDesk reports, so neither asset offered shelter.

For EU readers, a strong dollar means the fall looks slightly smaller in euro terms.
