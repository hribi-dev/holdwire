# How we make money

Tickora is free to read. These are the ways we pay for it:

1. **Sponsored content and press releases.** Companies pay to publish labelled pieces in separate sections. [Policy](/advertising-policy)
2. **Advertising.** Display ads on some pages, always labelled.
3. **Newsletter sponsorships.** A labelled sponsor slot in our newsletters.
4. **Affiliate links.** On some comparison pages, a provider may pay us if you open an account through our link. It costs you nothing extra, and it doesn't change our rankings. We only use paid links for providers authorised to serve readers in the EU; others get a plain, unpaid link. Affiliate links are marked "Affiliate link" and carry a sponsored-link tag for search engines.
5. **Reader tips.** Readers can tip us in crypto if they like what we do. Tips buy nothing: no coverage, no influence.

None of these buys editorial coverage. If a company pays us and is also in the news, our newsroom covers it like any other company and discloses the relationship.
