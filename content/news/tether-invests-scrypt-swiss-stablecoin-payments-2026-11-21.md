**Tether, the issuer of the USDT stablecoin, has made a strategic investment in SCRYPT, a Swiss stablecoin trading and liquidity provider, to help it move into payments for other businesses. The terms were not disclosed.**

SCRYPT already offers institutions liquidity, foreign exchange and cross-border settlement, with round-the-clock spot, OTC and FX trading and segregated custody in Switzerland, according to Fintechnews Switzerland. The new money is meant to fund its move into third-party payments for banks, brokers, fintechs and payment firms, The Cryptonomist reports. The idea: take in fiat money, move stablecoins across the border, and pay out fiat on the other side within minutes.

Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino said the aim is for money to move "at the speed of business, not the speed of banks", The Cryptonomist reports. SCRYPT founder and CEO Norman Wooding said Tether's backing supports the firm's expansion into fiat accounts and payments.

The deal was announced on 19 November. For EU readers, note that USDT is not authorised under MiCA, and ESMA has given EU crypto platforms until early next year to stop offering such stablecoins. Business payment rails like this one can still use stablecoins behind the scenes, so watch which tokens they settle in.
