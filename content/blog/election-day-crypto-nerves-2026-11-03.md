**Short version:** Americans vote in the midterm elections today, and crypto is nervous. Bitcoin slipped below $77,000, investors pulled about $1 billion from US Bitcoin ETFs over two sessions, and the Crypto Fear & Greed Index fell to 26. Options traders are paying up to protect themselves against a big move around the results.

## What happened

Bitcoin fell about 2% over the past day to around $76,500–76,800, after briefly recovering towards $78,000 on Monday. Ether is near $2,250. Tech stocks are weaker too: the Nasdaq fell about 1% in the last session.

Three things are weighing on prices:

- **ETF outflows.** US spot Bitcoin ETFs lost $398.6 million on 2 November, after $643.3 million on 30 October.
- **Election uncertainty.** Control of Congress will shape the crypto laws still waiting for a vote.
- **The Binance hack hangover.** The exchange has reopened withdrawals and says it is covering the $6.3 billion loss, but traders are still cautious. [Our explainer on the hack](/blog/binance-hack-what-it-means-2026-11-02) has the details.

## Traders are paying for protection

Options give a useful read on expectations. According to KuCoin's daily report, implied volatility on Bitcoin options expiring 4 November, the day after the vote, rose to about 43.5%, compared with about 36% for later expiries. In plain words: traders expect a bigger-than-usual move as results come in, but they don't agree on which way.

The Fear & Greed Index, a popular sentiment gauge, dropped to 26 from 37, firmly in "fear".

## What history says (and doesn't)

Analysts like to point out that Bitcoin fell sharply in previous midterm years (2014, 2018 and 2022), and that markets often recovered in the following months. A Binance Research report notes the S&P 500 has averaged about 19% in the 12 months after midterms.

Treat this carefully. There are only three Bitcoin midterm cycles, each happened at a different point in the market, and past patterns are not a forecast. The honest takeaway is that election weeks tend to be volatile, not that any direction is guaranteed.

## What this means for you

- **Expect bigger swings for a few days.** If you trade with leverage, wider moves mean a higher chance of being liquidated. Check your margin before the results land tonight (US time), which is early Wednesday morning in Europe.
- **Don't react to the first headline.** Election results come in over hours, sometimes days. Early moves often reverse.
- **Long-term holders can ignore most of this.** A single election doesn't change how Bitcoin works.

## What to watch this week

- **3 November:** US midterm elections. Results come through overnight European time.
- **4 November:** Bitcoin options expiry (see above) and US services PMI.
- **5 November:** Bank of England rate decision.
- **6 November:** US jobs report for October, and a Hyperliquid team token release of about 180,000 HYPE (roughly 0.08% of supply). HYPE is trading near $83–85 today.
- **Binance:** the exchange promised a full proof-of-reserves report showing it holds enough assets to cover customer balances. We'll cover it when it's out.
