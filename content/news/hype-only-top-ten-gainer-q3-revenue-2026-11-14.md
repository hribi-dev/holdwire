**Hyperliquid's HYPE token rose 6.43% to $74.32 by 13:16 UTC on Friday, 13 November, according to TokenPost, while bitcoin fell below $70,000 and ether, BNB and XRP all lost ground.**

CryptoTicker, republished by KuCoin News, said HYPE was the only coin among the ten largest trading higher over 24 hours. The bounce came after a weak stretch: HYPE was still down a little more than 5% on the week and about 24% below its record of almost $98 set in September. Earlier this week it hit [a four-week low](/news/hype-four-week-low-despite-korean-listings-2026-11-11).

The same day, a quarterly research report on Hyperliquid, summarised by The Crypto Basic, put third-quarter protocol revenue at $190.9 million. That is 21.7% more than in the second quarter but 36.5% less than a year earlier. Open positions on the exchange were worth $16.8 billion at the end of September, almost 80% more than three months before, and HIP-3 markets, those launched by outside teams, traded $153.8 billion in the quarter.

Most of Hyperliquid's trading fees go to its Assistance Fund, which buys HYPE on the market. Because it buys a dollar amount, it gets more tokens when the price is lower, CryptoTicker notes. Those buybacks depend on trading volume, and they do not stop HYPE from falling.
