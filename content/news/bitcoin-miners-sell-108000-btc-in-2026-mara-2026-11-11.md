**Fourteen publicly listed companies have sold a combined 108,247 bitcoin, worth about $8.08 billion, so far in 2026, according to on-chain analytics firm Lookonchain, cited by CoinDesk.**

Most of it comes from two miners. Lookonchain counts 40,581 BTC sold by MARA Holdings at an average price of $74,446, and 20,264 BTC sold by Riot Platforms at an average of $73,231. Together that is about 56% of the total. CoinDesk separately reports that MARA sold 13,597 BTC, for about $1.1 billion, over the past six weeks, and says it plans to sell more.

Miners earn new bitcoin for securing the network and have always sold some of it to pay for power and equipment. What has changed is the scale. Several large miners are also turning their sites into data centres for AI computing, and Riot signed a large AI data-centre lease last week.

The selling adds supply at a time when US spot bitcoin ETFs are also seeing outflows: about $1.5 billion since 2 November, CoinDesk reports. Company sales are one of several pressures on the price, not the only one.
