**Bitcoin fell 2.4% to about $62,450 late on Wednesday, 18 November, and ether dropped 2.4% to $1,754, according to CoinDesk, as signs of more US rate rises pushed the dollar higher.**

Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC that the Fed may need to raise rates by 0.5 percentage points in December, twice the move markets expect, Bitcoin World reports. He pointed to three months of 0.3% monthly core inflation, CoinDesk reports, and said businesses have grown used to raising prices by 3% or more a year.

The minutes of the Fed's October meeting, published the same afternoon, showed that nearly all policymakers expected a December rate rise and two raised the possibility of a half-point move, Kitco reports. Futures priced about a 95% chance of at least a 0.25-point rise at the 8–9 December meeting. The dollar index rose towards its highest level since April 2025.

Bitcoin is down 15.5% in seven days, Bitcoin World reports. CryptoQuant data cited by CoinDesk shows short-term holders sitting on their deepest losses since July, about 20% below what they paid. Strategy shares fell 4% and its STRC preferred stock dropped to its lowest since August, CoinDesk reports.

For EU readers, a stronger dollar softens the fall in euro terms.
