**A perpetual futures contract tracking Nvidia's share price on Hyperliquid rose as much as 4.5% on Sunday, briefly reaching $166.97, after the Financial Times reported that Jensen Huang has discussed returning as Nvidia's chief executive, according to Binance News.**

Huang stepped down on Thursday, 13 November. According to the FT, as relayed by Yahoo Finance, he has talked with confidants about coming back, and Silicon Valley executives are organising a push for his return. The FT also reported that Nvidia's board had approached Arm CEO Rene Haas about the job. Chief financial officer Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia has not commented on the report, and no board decision has been reported.

Because the Nasdaq was closed, the Hyperliquid contract was one of the few places where traders could react straight away. Gulf News put the Sunday gain at more than 3%, and said Nvidia shares were little changed, around $162.49, in early trading after the weekend. Nvidia shares fell more than 12% last week, Yahoo Finance reports, and the company reports quarterly results on Tuesday, 17 November.

For EU readers: these contracts trade around the clock, so they can react to weekend news before the stock does. They are leveraged derivatives, not shares, and a rumour-driven move can reverse just as fast when the real market opens.
