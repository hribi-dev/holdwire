**Short version:** After Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's chief executive on Thursday, a perpetual futures contract tracking Nvidia's share price on Hyperliquid traded about $2.35 billion in 24 hours, almost twice as much as ether on the same exchange, according to CoinMarketCap. Most of that trading happened while Wall Street was closed. It is the clearest sign yet that crypto venues are becoming a place to trade stock news around the clock, though the extra volume has not yet shown up as much extra revenue for Hyperliquid.

## What happened

Nvidia said on Thursday, 12 November, that Huang had stepped down as president, chief executive and board member with immediate effect. Finance chief Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board looks for a permanent successor, according to the company's announcement as reported by crypto.news. His departure followed an internal investigation and a week of media reports about his conduct, The Block reports. Nvidia's shares opened down 12%.

On Hyperliquid, traders had been reacting since Wednesday evening, when reports first said the board was weighing a change. We covered that first move [in a news brief](/news/nvidia-perps-hyperliquid-record-open-interest-2026-11-12).

The numbers from the following 24 hours, according to Hyperliquid data cited by CoinMarketCap:

- The Nvidia contract traded about $960 million on 11 November and about $1.7 billion on 12 November by midday (UTC days). Both were records, and both were more than ether traded on Hyperliquid.
- In the 24 hours to 12:10 New York time on Thursday, volume reached $2.35 billion. Its busiest day before this, since September, was $317 million on 5 November.
- About 62% of that volume traded outside regular US stock-market hours, by CoinMarketCap's calculation.
- The contract and the stock both bottomed near $149 when Nasdaq opened on Thursday, then recovered much of the drop by midday.
- Open interest, the value of positions still open, was near $495 million. A snapshot before the US open, cited by crypto.news, showed $531.2 million.

Big traders took both sides. On-chain tracker Lookonchain reported one wallet holding a long position equal to 60,000 Nvidia shares, worth about $9.35 million at up to 20 times leverage, and adding buy orders below the market, crypto.news reports. That wallet was showing a loss of about $200,000 at the time.

## Why it matters

The contract is one of Hyperliquid's HIP-3 markets: markets that outside teams can launch on the exchange. This one is run by trade.xyz. It is not a share; it is a leveraged derivative that follows Nvidia's price and trades 24 hours a day, every day.

That is the point. When big company news lands after the stock market closes, the stock itself cannot be traded in size until the next morning. Hyperliquid offered a place to react straight away, and traders used it. CoinMarketCap also reports that stock-linked markets on trade.xyz together outtraded all of Hyperliquid's crypto markets over the same 24 hours.

For Hyperliquid's own token, HYPE, the effect is less direct. Part of each fee on HIP-3 markets goes first to the team that runs the market, according to Hyperliquid's documentation cited by CoinMarketCap. HIP-3 markets made up about 4% of Hyperliquid's 30-day protocol revenue as of 7 November, according to GLC Research. Protocol revenue, which funds HYPE buybacks, was $2.1 million on 11 November, below the previous week's high of $3.6 million, according to DefiLlama. HYPE traded at $71.00 on Thursday afternoon, down almost 10% in a week.

## What this means for you

- **These are not shares.** A stock perpetual gives you price exposure with leverage, but no ownership, voting rights or dividends, and positions can be liquidated in minutes when news hits.
- **EU protections do not follow you there.** Hyperliquid is a decentralised exchange, not an authorised EU venue, and leveraged derivatives fall under EU investment rules rather than [MiCA](/learn/mica-explained). The safeguards you would get at a regulated EU broker, such as limits on retail leverage, do not apply.
- **Volume is not the same as value.** A record trading day for one market does not automatically mean more income for HYPE holders, as the revenue figures above show.
- **Funding costs add up.** Perpetuals charge or pay funding every hour on Hyperliquid. On a large position held for days, that cost matters.

## What to watch

- Whether stock-linked trading stays high once the Nvidia story calms down, or fades back to normal levels.
- Whether higher volume lifts Hyperliquid's protocol revenue and the buybacks it funds, which CoinMarketCap calls the next test.
- The US fight over stock perpetuals, where [Coinbase, Kalshi and Kraken's parent want to join a court case on the CFTC's side](/news/coinbase-kalshi-kraken-back-cftc-stock-perps-lawsuit-2026-11-11).
