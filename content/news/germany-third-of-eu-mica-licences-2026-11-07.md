**Germany accounts for 122 of the 394 crypto-asset service providers licensed under MiCA in ESMA's register, almost a third of the EU total, according to a report published on 7 November.**

France is second with 36 licensed firms and the Netherlands third with 29. Germany's count has more than doubled this year: from 53 entities in April to 57 at the end of June and 79 in August, the report says. Many of the new names are local cooperative banks, such as Raiffeisenbank Eifel (authorised 19 October) and Volksbank Niederrhein (22 October).

Germany's financial regulator BaFin said the high number reflects the size of the country's financial sector and its many credit institutions, and that Germany already had a national crypto licensing regime before MiCA. Totals differ between trackers: one, Penning, counted 387 authorisations in effect EU-wide (117 German) as of 6 November, because ESMA's register also lists some licences that start later in November.

For comparison, crypto.news counted 244 valid MiCA licences on 29 June, just before the 1 July deadline. For EU users, the practical point is that a licence follows the company, not the brand: check that the exact entity you sign up with is in the register. A licence also allows a firm to serve customers across the EU, so a German or Dutch provider can be an option for users elsewhere.
