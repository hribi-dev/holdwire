**Ethereum Foundation researcher Justin Drake said at the opening of Devcon 8 in Mumbai that he hopes Ethereum can finish its "full post-quantum and post-AI transition" by December 2028, TokenPost reports.**

## What was said

That is a year earlier than the Ethereum Foundation's published plan, which sets December 2029 as a self-imposed target for making the network's execution, consensus and data layers resistant to quantum computers. According to TokenPost, Drake described 2028 as a hope, not a commitment, and it does not replace the official target. The Foundation plans to review progress on quantum computing again in January 2027.

Vitalik Buterin raised a related worry on the same day. He wrote that there may be undiscovered shortcuts for breaking the maths that protects Ethereum and Bitcoin private keys, and that AI could find them faster than expected, KuCoin News reports. No such shortcut is known today.

## Why it matters

Today's crypto wallets rely on signature schemes that a large enough quantum computer could in theory break. Moving a network the size of Ethereum to new cryptography takes years, so the timing of the work matters even though no such machine exists yet.

## What it means for you

Nothing changes for your wallet today, and there is nothing to do. If the upgrade comes, users may at some point need to move funds to new, quantum-safe accounts. Treat any message telling you to "migrate now" as a likely scam until official Ethereum channels announce the steps.
