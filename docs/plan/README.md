## The one-paragraph plan

Build a fast, technically perfect Next.js site with a headless CMS. Win search by owning **topics**, not just publishing news. CoinMarketCap's real moat is its **data pages** (one page per asset, updated live), not its blog. We copy that: data/reference pages for crypto assets, fintech companies, watch references and collectibles categories, surrounded by guides, glossaries and timely news. Earn authority with original data and tools that other sites cite. Open sponsored slots only after real traffic exists (~6 months in). Sell **audience reach and brand visibility**, never "SEO juice". Every paid link gets `rel="sponsored"` and every paid post gets a clear label. Google penalizes sites that rent out their ranking power, and that one rule decides whether this business works long-term.
