**Spot gold fell $125.90, or 3.27%, to about $3,722.60 an ounce late on Wednesday, 18 November, its lowest level since September 2025, Kitco reports. Silver lost 4.95% to about $44.93.**

The trigger was the Federal Reserve. Minutes of its October meeting showed nearly all policymakers expected to raise rates in December, with two raising the possibility of a 0.5-point rise, according to Kitco. Governor Christopher Waller had already called for a half-point hike that morning, CoinDesk reports. Higher rates make gold, which pays no interest, less attractive, and the dollar index climbed towards its highest since April 2025.

The drop came a day after gold had steadied near $3,853. It has now erased all of its 2026 gains, according to BeInCrypto. Rising tension between the US and Iran brought no safe-haven buying, Kitco notes. Silver fell harder on worries about industrial demand, and copper also slid to its weakest since April, CoinDesk reports.

For EU readers, gold is priced in dollars, so the strong dollar softened the fall in euro terms.
