**Gnosis Pay, which issues crypto debit cards in Europe, says attackers gained unauthorised access to customer data on the legacy system behind its own-brand cards, detected on 3 November 2026, according to CryptoTicker.**

The company says it contained the access within two hours, rotated the affected credentials and closed the vulnerability. It has not said how many customers were affected or listed every type of data taken; affected customers were emailed details of what was exposed from their accounts. Its white-label card platform, used by partner apps, was not affected, the company says.

Gnosis Pay says customer funds were not touched. Its accounts are self-custodial on the blockchain, so the breach did not expose keys, according to the company. The bigger risk now is follow-up fraud: stolen personal data makes fake emails, texts and calls look convincing.

The company's advice, as reported by CryptoTicker: never share your seed phrase, private keys, PIN or full card number; make account changes only in the official web app or partner app, not through links; approve only 3D Secure requests you started yourself; and verify contact by typing help@gnosispay.com in manually.

Separately, Gnosis Pay's consumer card system is due to shut down on 20 December 2026, so own-brand cardholders need another card by then.
