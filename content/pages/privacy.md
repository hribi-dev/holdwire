# Privacy policy

We collect as little as we can. This page explains what happens to your data when you read Tickora.

## Who is responsible

The controller is the publisher of Tickora: [publisher name, address and company registration number]. Contact for privacy questions: [privacy@domain].

## What we don't do

- **No tracking cookies and no analytics.** We don't use Google Analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
- **No fonts or scripts from third parties on page load.** Our fonts are hosted on our own servers.
- **We don't sell or share your data** with advertisers. Sponsors never get information about our readers.

## What happens when you visit

- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) processes technical data needed to deliver the site, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and keeps it for a short time for security and error analysis. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Images.** Article cover photos are loaded from Unsplash's image servers (images.unsplash.com), which receive your IP address when your browser loads a photo.
- **Settings stored in your browser.** If you choose a dark or light theme, take School lessons, or hide the sponsor sticker, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device; we never receive it. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

## Posts from X (Twitter)

On the Market movers page, posts from X are not loaded automatically. Only when you click "Show the post" does your browser connect to X, which may set cookies and process your data under [X's privacy policy](https://x.com/en/privacy). Until you click, X receives nothing from us.

## Market data

Prices come from CoinGecko, Hyperliquid and GeckoTerminal. Our servers fetch this data; your browser does not contact these services and they receive no data about you.

## When you contact us

If you email us (for corrections, advertising or anything else), we use your email address and message only to reply and keep a record of the conversation. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or steps before a contract for advertising enquiries.

## Newsletter

When our newsletter launches, signing up will require your consent. We will name the email provider here before collecting any address, and every email will include an unsubscribe link.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a copy of your data, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to [privacy@domain]. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (Informacijski pooblaščenec, ip-rs.si).

## Changes

We will update this page when we add new services, such as the newsletter or analytics, and note the date below.
