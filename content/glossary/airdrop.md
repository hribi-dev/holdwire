---
title: "Airdrop"
short: "Free tokens distributed to a group of wallets, often early users of a project."
published: 2026-11-02
author: Tickora
---
Projects use airdrops to reward early users and spread ownership. Hyperliquid's HYPE token launched with an airdrop in November 2024. Airdrops can be taxable income in some EU countries.
